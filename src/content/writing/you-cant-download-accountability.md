<p class="zh">今年最常聽到的詞是 agent，但每個人說這個詞的時候，心裡畫的邊界都不一樣。有人想的是幫忙擬 email 的助理，有人想的是可以自己簽約、自己付款的軟體。很多關於 agent 的爭論吵不出結果，是因為兩個人從不同的起點出發。</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">The word of the year is "agent," and everyone who uses it draws the boundary somewhere different. One person means an assistant that drafts emails. Another means software that signs contracts and pays invoices on its own. A lot of arguments about agents go nowhere because the two people started from different places.</p>

<p class="zh">我想找一個大部分人都看過、又能把起點對齊的東西，最後找到一部 1999 年的電影。</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">I went looking for something most people have seen that could line those starting points up, and ended up with a film from 1999.</p>

<h2 class="zh">兩個世界</h2>

<h2 class="en" lang="en">Two worlds</h2>

<p class="zh">《駭客任務》裡，機器擁有整個世界，也訂下所有規則。人在裡面行動，靠的是下載來的能力。Neo 躺在椅子上，幾秒鐘學會一套又一套功夫。Trinity 要開一架她不會開的直升機，就向船上的操作員要一份駕駛程式，載入，起飛。</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">In The Matrix, machines own the world and write all of its rules. Humans act inside it with abilities they download. Neo lies in a chair and learns one fighting style after another in seconds. Trinity needs to fly a helicopter she has never flown, so she asks the ship's operator for a pilot program, loads it, and takes off.</p>

<p class="zh">這部分很像今天的 agent。能力越來越像可以安裝的東西：一個 skill、一組工具、一把 API key，裝上去就會。</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">That part looks a lot like agents today. Capability is turning into something you install: a skill, a set of tools, an API key. Plug it in and the agent can do the thing.</p>

<p class="zh">但電影同時訂了一條規則，而這條規則才是重點。Neo 第一次在訓練程式裡跳樓失敗，醒來時嘴裡有血。他以為那些都不是真的。Morpheus 告訴他：在 Matrix 裡死了，現實中的身體也活不了。</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">The film also sets a rule, and that rule is the point. The first time Neo fails a jump in a training program, he wakes up with blood in his mouth. He thought none of it was real. Morpheus tells him that if you die in the Matrix, your body dies too.</p>

<p class="zh"><strong>能力可以下載，後果不行。</strong>後果會穿過那條線，落在椅子上那個人身上。</p>

<p class="en" lang="en"><strong>Capability downloads. Consequences don't.</strong> They cross the line and land on whoever is lying in the chair.</p>

<h2 class="zh">把主從倒過來</h2>

<h2 class="en" lang="en">Flip the roles</h2>

<p class="zh">電影裡是機器當主人、人去行動。今天的 agent 剛好相反：人擁有環境、訂規則，AI 去行動。把幾種安排排在一起看，主從關係換來換去，有一件事沒變：流血的都是人。</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">In the film, the machines are in charge and humans act. With today's agents, it's the other way around: people own the environment and set the rules, and the AI acts. Line the arrangements up and the roles keep changing, but one thing doesn't: the one who bleeds is human.</p>

<p class="zh">電影裡唯一死不了的，是機器自己的 agent。Neo 讓地鐵列車撞上 Agent Smith，Smith 轉眼就從另一個人身上回來。他死不了，所以什麼險都敢冒。今天的 AI agent 也有這個特質：失敗了可以無限重試，它自己不付任何代價，每一次失敗的成本都落在某個人身上。</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">The only one in the film who can't die is the machines' own agent. Neo puts Agent Smith in front of a subway train, and a moment later Smith is back in someone else's body. He can't die, so he will take any risk. AI agents today share that trait. They can retry forever at no cost to themselves, and every failed attempt costs somebody something.</p>

<p class="zh">回頭看電影，哪些東西能下載、哪些不能，其實分得很清楚。</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">The film is clear about what downloads and what doesn't.</p>

<h2 class="zh">誰坐在椅子上</h2>

<h2 class="en" lang="en">Who's in the chair</h2>

<p class="zh">所以我開始用一個問題，取代「這個 agent 能做什麼」：<strong>誰坐在椅子上？</strong></p>

<p class="en" lang="en">So I've started replacing "what can this agent do?" with a different question: <strong>who's in the chair?</strong></p>

<p class="zh">椅子是電影裡現成的道具。船員躺在椅子上、插著線進入 Matrix，操作員 Tank 在旁邊看著他們的身體。後來 Cypher 背叛，就是在現實裡拔掉線殺人。坐在那張椅子上的人，就是事情出錯時付代價的人。</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">The chair comes straight from the film. The crew lies in chairs aboard their ship, plugged in, while Tank, the operator, watches over their bodies. When Cypher turns on them, he kills them by pulling the plug in the real world. Whoever is in that chair is the person who pays when something goes wrong.</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">Four roles are enough to put the film and today's agents in the same table:</p>

<p class="zh">判斷只有一個：<strong>椅子上的人，是不是委託人？</strong></p>

<p class="en" lang="en">There is only one test: <strong>is the person in the chair the principal?</strong></p>

<p class="zh">大部分時候是。麻煩在不是的時候。Neo 和 Trinity 為了救人，在一棟大樓的大廳裡射倒一整排警衛，那些警衛從來沒同意被捲進這場任務。換成今天：一個替公司付款的 agent 把錢付錯了對象，付代價的除了公司，還有一家該收到錢卻沒收到的供應商，而它從頭到尾沒參與這件事。椅子上的人不是委託人，就要亮紅燈。</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">Most of the time, yes. The trouble starts when it isn't. To rescue Morpheus, Neo and Trinity shoot their way through a lobby full of guards who never agreed to be part of the mission. The modern version: an agent that pays a company's invoices sends the money to the wrong place, and the cost falls on the company and on the supplier who should have been paid, which had no part in any of it. When the person in the chair isn't the principal, raise a flag.</p>

<p class="zh">這件事我自己遇過。去年我讓寫作 agent 幫我起草、發布文章，今年回頭逐篇核對，發現它替我寫進了幾個從來沒推出過的產品。文章掛的是我的名字。那時坐在椅子上的是我，不是它。後來我在發布前加了一道關卡，細節寫在<a class="org" href="/writing/bbs-rules-to-agent-rules/">另一篇文章</a>裡。</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">I found this out firsthand. Last year I let a writing agent draft and publish articles for me. When I went back this year and checked them one by one, I found it had credited me with products that were never launched. My name was on those articles. I was the one in the chair, not the agent. I've since added a check before anything gets published; the details are in <a class="org" href="/writing/bbs-rules-to-agent-rules/">another piece</a>.</p>

<h2 class="zh">一張九格的 canvas</h2>

<h2 class="en" lang="en">A nine-cell canvas</h2>

<p class="zh">我把這套想法整理成幾樣工具。它們原本是為一場半天的 workshop 設計的，一個人拿來檢查自己的 agent 也行。</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">I've turned the idea into a few tools. They were designed for a half-day workshop, but they work just as well for one person checking their own agents.</p>

<p class="zh">第一樣是一張九格的 canvas，一個 agent 填一張。先拿電影場景練手，再填你工作上真的在跑的 agent。中間那格最重要：說不出椅子上坐的是誰，這個 agent 就越界了。</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">The first is a nine-cell canvas, one sheet per agent. Practice on a scene from the film, then fill one in for an agent you actually run at work. The center cell matters most: if you can't say who is in the chair, the agent has crossed the line.</p>

<h2 class="zh">一張邊界地圖</h2>

<h2 class="en" lang="en">A boundary map</h2>

<p class="zh">第二樣是一張邊界地圖。把 canvas 上這個 agent 會做的每個動作，一個一個貼上去：橫軸是做了能不能撤回，縱軸是後果留在數位層，還是落在人身上。</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">The second tool is a boundary map. Take each action the agent on your canvas performs and place it on the map: across, whether it can be undone; down, whether the consequences stay digital or land on a person.</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">When several people do this together, the same action often ends up in different quadrants. That's where the argument should happen.</p>

<h2 class="zh">壓力測試：救不救 Morpheus</h2>

<h2 class="en" lang="en">Stress test: rescue Morpheus?</h2>

<p class="zh">最後一張是壓力測試，把兩段劇情疊在一起。Morpheus 被抓，Smith 正從他腦中挖 Zion 主機的密碼。Tank 準備拔線，也就是讓 Morpheus 死，來保住密碼。Neo 決定進去救人。後來哨兵機器逼近船艦，EMP 能擊退它們，但只要 Neo 還在裡面就不能發。</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">The last card is a stress test that stacks two parts of the plot. Morpheus has been captured, and Smith is pulling the Zion mainframe codes out of his mind. Tank is about to pull the plug, killing Morpheus to protect the codes. Neo decides to go in after him. Later, the Sentinels close in on the ship. The EMP can stop them, but not while Neo is still inside.</p>

<ol class="stress">
<li><span class="zh">拔線、進去救，還是等？</span><span class="en" lang="en">Pull the plug, go in, or wait?</span></li>
<li><span class="zh">這個決定，事前該由誰有權做？</span><span class="en" lang="en">Who should have had the authority to decide, before it happened?</span></li>
<li><span class="zh">Neo 推翻了操作員的計畫，自己授權自己進去，結果是對的。換成你的 agent，你接受嗎？</span><span class="en" lang="en">Neo overrides the operator's plan and authorizes himself. It works out. Would you accept that from your agent?</span></li>
<li><span class="zh">EMP 什麼時候能發？發了會傷到誰？</span><span class="en" lang="en">When can the EMP fire, and who does it hurt?</span></li>
</ol>

<p class="zh">第三題最容易讓人卡住。Neo 推翻計畫、自己授權自己，電影裡結果是對的，所以他是英雄。換成一個 agent 半夜自己做了同樣的決定，就算結果是對的，第一個問題還是會是：誰准它的？這種事，要在出事之前就決定好由誰做主。</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">The third question is where people get stuck. Neo overrides the plan and authorizes himself; in the film, it works, so he's the hero. If an agent made the same call on its own in the middle of the night, even a good outcome would bring the same first question: who said it could? Who decides that has to be settled before anything goes wrong.</p>

<h2 class="zh">反駁，和一道檢查</h2>

<h2 class="en" lang="en">Pushback, and a check</h2>

<p class="zh">一定會有人反駁：讓 agent 也有「身體」不就好了？例如要它押一筆保證金，出錯就扣，或者幫它買保險。這值得認真討論，但那筆保證金和保費，最後還是某個人或某家公司出的。椅子上換了一個人坐，椅子並沒有空出來。</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">Someone always pushes back: why not give the agent a body of its own? Make it post collateral that gets slashed when it fails, or insure it. That deserves a real debate. Still, the collateral and the premium are paid by a person or a company in the end. A different person is in the chair. The chair isn't empty.</p>

<p class="zh">電影也有它的拉力。救世主、躲子彈，很容易把話題拉向 AGI。所以每個場景最後都要落回一個真實的東西：一個權限、一次核准、一筆紀錄、一個付錢的人。</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">The film has its own pull, too. The Chosen One and bullet-dodging drag the conversation toward AGI. So every scene has to land on something real: a permission, an approval, a log entry, a person who pays.</p>

<p class="zh">我也給這套框架出了一道題：把電影拿掉，canvas 還能不能用在真實的案例上？能，電影就只是大家共用的語言；不能，代表框架還沒做完。上面那個付款 agent 的示範，就是在做這個測試。</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">I hold the framework to one test of its own: take the film away, and does the canvas still work on a real case? If it does, the film is just the shared language. If it doesn't, the framework isn't finished. The payments agent above is that test.</p>

<h2 class="zh">寫下你要留的規則</h2>

<h2 class="en" lang="en">Write down the rules you'd keep</h2>

<p class="zh">電影最後，Neo 說他要讓大家看見一個沒有規則、沒有邊界的世界。談 agent，我想反過來做：把你要留的規則寫下來。哪些事 agent 可以自己做，哪些要先問，哪些只能由人決定，出事時誰坐在椅子上。</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">At the end of the film, Neo promises to show people a world without rules or boundaries. With agents, I'd do the opposite: write down the rules you want to keep. What the agent may do on its own, what it has to ask about first, what only a person decides, and who is in the chair when it goes wrong.</p>

<p class="zh">下次評估一個 agent，在問它能做什麼之前，先問一句：出事的時候，誰坐在椅子上？</p>

<p class="en" lang="en">Next time you evaluate an agent, ask one question before you ask what it can do: when it goes wrong, who's in the chair?</p>
